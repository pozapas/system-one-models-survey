# Adjudication of the version 1.2 second rating

Source: Adjudication_v12_returned.xlsx. Every disagreement between the first rating and the second rating was decided by the author against the full text. Rows whose final value equals the first rating are not listed.

## Rules

| rule | domain | decision | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Version pinning and run dates | reject | Reject the mandatory hash or dated-snapshot requirement. PROTOCOL.md permits an exact model version OR revision, together with run dates. Low requires this information for every compared system. Incomplete versions or missing dates remain unclear. A missing version identifier or an unresolved rolling alias remains high. Rechecked all 41 linked rows. Their other gaps preserve their final ratings. |
| G2 | Version pinning and run dates | accept | Accept. Apply the version anchor to every compared system that contributes to the results, including baselines and scoring judges. Training-only teachers do not enter this comparison. Source: PROTOCOL.md, Risk of bias, domain 3. Second_rating_guide.md, anchor notes. |
| G3 | Test-set exposure | accept | Accept. A 2026 public release alone does not establish exclusion from model training. Use unclear for that gap. Use low for new author-built items or private items. Source: PROTOCOL.md, Risk of bias, domain 5. |
| G4 | Test-set exposure | accept | Accept. A relevant partial overlap check removes the no-check condition for high but does not establish low risk. Residual exposure remains unclear. Source: PROTOCOL.md, Risk of bias, domain 5. |
| G5 | Reference-label independence | accept | Accept. Distinguish an observed action outcome from a stored synthetic reference answer. Apply unclear when headline results mix reference types. Source: PROTOCOL.md, Risk of bias, domain 1. Second_rating_guide.md, anchor notes. |
| C1 | Sampling and reporting | accept | Accept. Seed SD or SE alone is not an interval on a headline result or contrast. A confirmed count contradiction is high. Rounded percentages alone do not prove a count contradiction. Source: PROTOCOL.md, Risk of bias, domain 2. See RB084. |
| C2 | Tuning-budget parity | accept | Accept for model settings selected with evaluation feedback. Disclosure or equal application does not remove test tuning. Use unclear for actual disclosed and bounded differences in training, instructions or budget. A predeclared descriptive operating-point metric alone does not establish tuning. Source: PROTOCOL.md, Risk of bias, domain 4. See RB038. |
| C3 | Reference-label independence | accept | Accept. Use the provenance reported in the study. Missing provenance remains unclear. Headline results with different reference types remain unclear. Source: PROTOCOL.md, Risk of bias, domain 1. |
| C4 | Test-set exposure | accept | Accept. The version 1.2 addition explicitly assigns unclear to a mixture of new items and public pre-2026 sets. It specifies no minimum share. Source: PROTOCOL.md, Version 1.2. |
