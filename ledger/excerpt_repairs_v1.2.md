## Causes found and how they were repaired

- Table captions were quoted as "Table N." where the saved text has "Table N:" (or the reverse), and the checker keeps full stops but drops colons. Captions are now copied exactly.
- The first row label was fused to the caption without a separator. Caption, header cells and row cells are now joined with " | ".
- Coder annotations written inside the excerpt (for example "(NLP columns)", "Agent: ... / Compressor: ...", "row +SJC (Jev), Average (168) Pass3 = 73.2", "[Zero-shot rows, SR column: ...]") are not source text. They were removed; column and block identification now lives in the location field and in the copied header and block-label cells.
- Percent signs written as "\%" in the saved text of 2610.20927 were typed as "%" in the excerpts. The excerpts now carry the text exactly as saved.
- Row labels that carry zero-width spaces, non-breaking spaces or math-alphanumeric letters (2610.18389 alpha-PRISM row, 2609.34969 "P2DNav [22]") are copied from the saved file. Non-breaking spaces are written as ordinary spaces.
- Cells that the coders had abbreviated or reordered (for example "Latency 051", "Jev -> GPT-5.5", "Target 3.9, Switched 9.3") are now the cells as they appear in the text.
- A line-end hyphenation in the 2610.28826 text (L4603) is kept as a space.
